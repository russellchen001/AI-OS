# AI-OS Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Product vision
**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Authority

This document owns brand and vision. `AI_OS_MASTER_GUIDE.md` owns product direction, architecture, roadmap, and scope and prevails on conflict. UI, visual, and design-delivery details belong to their respective companion specifications.

## Mission

Make useful AI capability accessible to ordinary people by turning natural-language goals into understandable, safe, and verifiable outcomes.

## Target Users

People who want help with knowledge work and real-world digital tasks without learning AI models, APIs, automation systems, or developer tooling. Advanced controls remain available without defining the default experience.

## Problem

AI capability is fragmented across chats, providers, tools, agents, files, and workflows. Users must often translate their goal into technical choices and manually coordinate the result. AI-OS takes responsibility for that complexity while preserving visibility and control.

## Core Promise

**Describe the outcome. AI-OS organizes the intelligence, capabilities, permissions, execution, and verification required to help achieve it.**

## Product Pillars

- **Ask and Do:** answer clearly or complete a permitted task.
- **Workspace:** conversations, tasks, approvals, and artifacts form one continuous experience.
- **AI independence:** providers and models are replaceable resources, not the product identity.
- **Local-first, not local-only:** prefer user ownership, privacy, resilience, and local capability while using cloud services when valuable and permitted.
- **Trustworthy execution:** consequential actions are permissioned, observable, recoverable, and verified.
- **Memory:** useful context reduces repetition while remaining inspectable and controllable.
- **Skills:** modular capabilities expand what AI-OS can do without turning the core into a collection of scripts.
- **AI Council:** dynamic expert collaboration improves decision quality.
- **AI Arena:** controlled multi-AI interaction enables exploration, experimentation, entertainment, and research.

## AI Council / AI 智囊团

**AI 智囊团——一个人，也能拥有整个专家团队。**

Council dynamically assembles relevant perspectives around a user decision. A Chief of Staff understands the goal, proposes the team, facilitates expert discussion, surfaces disagreement, and delivers a useful recommendation. Council is an organization and decision experience, not a static persona shelf or model leaderboard.

## AI Arena / AI 竞技场

Arena lets AIs interact under shared rules: compare, debate, collaborate, compete, role-play, play games, and simulate. Users can configure, observe, participate, vote, score, and replay. It is both playful and rigorous, preserving reproducible records where evaluation matters. It is not a substitute for Council decision support.

## Memory and Skills

Memory makes the workspace continuous across time without silently owning user intent or execution. Skills connect intent to real capabilities under explicit manifests and permission boundaries. Together they allow personalization and expansion without provider lock-in.

## Competitive Differentiation

AI-OS differentiates through the combination of conversational simplicity, task execution, replaceable AI, local-first ownership, dynamic expert decision support, controlled multi-AI interaction, and an integrated artifact/task workspace. These are product commitments, not claims about any named competitor.

## Brand Language

Preferred expressions:

- AI-OS — your personal AI operating system.
- Describe the outcome, not the tools.
- AI 智囊团——一个人，也能拥有整个专家团队。
- Your AI, agents, tasks, and results in one workspace.
- Local-first. Provider-independent. Under your control.

Voice is calm, capable, direct, and human. Avoid hype, anthropomorphic certainty, unexplained technical language, and claims that exceed implemented evidence.

## Non-Goals

AI-OS is not a replacement operating-system kernel, a provider-specific wrapper, a conventional developer dashboard, an ordinary workflow editor, a social network, or an excuse to automate consequential actions without consent. Entertainment in Arena does not turn the broader product into a gaming platform.

## Success Criteria

- Ordinary users can move from intent to verified result with less tool coordination.
- Users understand what is happening, what data or providers are involved, and when approval is required.
- Ask and Do journeys remain coherent across local and cloud capability.
- Providers, models, agents, and Skills can change without changing the user's mental model.
- Council produces transparent, decision-useful synthesis from dynamically selected expertise.
- Arena supports engaging and reproducible interaction across its required modes.
- Memory, permissions, artifacts, and recovery increase trust rather than hidden complexity.
