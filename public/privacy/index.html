AI-OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This policy describes how AI-OS handles information when a user chooses to connect an eBay account. It applies to the current AI-OS eBay integration.

What data AI-OS accesses

AI-OS uses eBay's official OAuth authorization flow and official APIs. Depending on the capabilities and scopes approved by eBay and granted by the user, AI-OS may request marketplace information needed for the action the user initiates, such as item discovery or other approved Buy API operations. AI-OS does not request access to unrelated eBay data.

How authentication works

Sign-in and authorization take place on eBay's official website. AI-OS does not ask for, receive, or store the user's eBay password. eBay returns OAuth access and refresh tokens after authorization. These tokens are treated as sensitive credentials and are stored only in the secure, encrypted credential storage used by the AI-OS connection service. They must not be placed in source code, git history, ordinary logs, or public pages.

Data storage and retention

The current integration calls eBay APIs as needed to complete a user-requested action. AI-OS does not currently maintain a persistent, long-term copy of eBay marketplace user data. OAuth credentials are kept only as required to maintain the authorized connection and are handled separately from marketplace data.

eBay data handling

Data returned by eBay is used only to provide the requested AI-OS capability and is not sold. The current no-long-term-copy design is the basis for AI-OS's Marketplace Account Deletion exemption. If AI-OS later begins persistently storing eBay user, order, transaction, or other marketplace data, this policy must be updated and the product must reassess its obligations for eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications before that change is released.

Revoking access

A user can disconnect eBay in AI-OS to delete the connection credentials held by the AI-OS connection service. A user can also revoke AI-OS access from the authorization settings in their eBay account. Revoking access may prevent eBay-connected features from working until the account is authorized again.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the AI-OS eBay integration or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
